Stadler GTW 2/6 (Řada 848)

Right now the work is mainly on the 3D model of the exterior for the unit itself. Work progress is planned out in this order:

  • Making 3D Model
  • Get 3D Printer and print test models before the finished version
  • Make electronics, including a custom working bogey for the "power car" in the middle, and working bogeys for the cab cars
  • test fit electronics, test drive
  • Paint the body
  • Assemble final product.

As of now the entire model has a rough body prepared to be made into the shape of the unit.
